## Natural Earth — `js/worldgeo.js`

World map geometry from [Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/)
1:110m admin-0 map units, generated by `tools/build-geo.js`. Natural Earth is in
the public domain; no attribution is required, and it is credited here anyway.
